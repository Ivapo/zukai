Zukai

A schematic road-network editor. It draws roundabouts, junctions and motorway segments as clean diagrams — not to scale, the way a metro map represents a transit network rather than a surveyed one.

Every figure on this page was drawn in Zukai and exported by it.

The label is the truth; the picture is a diagram

A road above carries the text 1800m, meaning this road is 1800 metres long. Retype it as 1500m and the drawing does not move. The two are deliberately decoupled: nothing computes a real-world quantity from canvas geometry, and nothing resizes the canvas to honour one. They are two independent records of the same road.

What it is not

  • Not to scale. Distances on the canvas are chosen for legibility. A road states its real length as an annotation.
  • Not a GIS tool. There is no basemap, no projection and no survey data. Zukai draws parts of networks — one interchange, one roundabout — rather than complete geography.
  • Not a simulator. Nothing here models traffic. A junction records what it permits; no vehicle ever uses it.
  • Not automatic. Importing populates the canvas; a human places and connects. Legible schematics come from human judgment, and fully automatic schematization is out of scope.

Reading Assimilator networks

Zukai reads the network.yaml format used by Assimilator, a to-scale microscopic traffic simulator developed separately. The coupling is one-way and the two share no code.

Import takes a network's topology — nodes, links, lanes, junctions, and which turns each junction permits — and discards its surveyed geometry, because a schematic distorts real geometry on purpose. Real lengths come across as labels rather than as distances. Zukai does not write that format; a network worth simulating is authored in Assimilator, where the geometry is real.

Get the desktop app

The browser demo is the whole editor, and it keeps nothing: documents leave the tab as files you download. The desktop build is the primary artifact — releases are published here, though no build has been cut yet, so that page is empty for now.
